Section 06
Conclusion & Strategic Summary

Key Takeaways

🟠 Temu

  • Massive influencer footprint (218 ambassadors)
  • 47M+ total follower reach — dominant scale
  • Female-leaning audience (60%) suits lifestyle/retail
  • Western markets lead: USA, Brazil, Italy
  • Mid-tier strategy (~216K avg followers)
  • Age 31.8 — core millennial consumer

🔴 Shopee

  • Lean, curated program (13 ambassadors)
  • 403K total reach — hyper-targeted niche
  • Balanced gender (50/50) — broad appeal
  • Taiwan primary market in Instagram data
  • Micro strategy (~31K avg followers)
  • Age 30.3 — slightly younger millennial
Analyst Note: Temu and Shopee represent two fundamentally different Instagram ambassador philosophies. Temu operates a wide-net, volume-driven program targeting Western markets at scale — optimized for brand awareness and impulse discovery. Shopee's lean program signals either a deliberate micro-influencer strategy for its Taiwan market, or that its primary influencer investment occurs on other platforms (TikTok, Shopee Live) native to its SEA user base. The 116× follower reach gap (47M vs 403K) is the most striking differentiator and reflects Temu's aggressive global expansion investment.
