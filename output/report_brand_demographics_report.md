## Key Insights:

### CELINE:
- More female-leaning audience (68.53%)
- Younger-skewing ambassador base
- Strong Korean market presence (home base ambassadors)
- More balanced international audience

### CHANEL:
- More male-leaning audience (72.74%)
- Slightly younger average age (29.65 vs 30.93 years)
- Stronger international diversity with significant German and American audiences
- Larger overall follower base (136.25M vs 121.37M)
- More global luxury appeal

### Data Notes:
- Some ambassadors lacked complete demographic data and were excluded from calculations
- Gender skew in Chanel may indicate data collection issues with private accounts or inactive profiles
- Demographic data represents estimated audience composition based on available Instagram analytics
