"/nls" - Search for creators across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok using natural language descriptions. The AI determines the appropriate platform from your query and converts it into structured search filters. Results are delivered via Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming.
"/youtube/search" - Search for YouTube creators using advanced filters such as `displayName`, subscriber count, video performance, and more. Supports fuzzy search, pagination, and multiple filter combinations with up to 10 filters per request. The `creatorList` is, by default, sorted by `displayName`. Custom sorting can be applied using the `sortBy` parameter.
"/youtube/profile" - Retrieve the profile information of a specified YouTube creator, including metadata such as subscriber count, account type, language, hashtags, and more.
"/youtube/contact" - Retrieve the contact information of the specified YouTube creator.
"/youtube/performance" - Retrieve advanced performance information of a specified YouTube creator, including upload activity, subscriber growth, engagement metrics, and video performance.
"/youtube/performance-history" - Retrieve daily performance snapshots for a specified YouTube creator, including upload activity, subscriber growth, engagement metrics, and video performance. Dates are returned as Unix timestamps in milliseconds.
"/youtube/audience" - Retrieve audience demographic insights for a specified YouTube creator, including country distribution, gender breakdown, and age composition. This endpoint provides detailed audience analytics to help understand creator reach, evaluate audience alignment, and support data‑driven influencer selection and campaign targeting.
"/youtube/content-detail" - Retrieve the most recent YouTube videos and shorts of a specified creator, including publish time, video or short description, and engagement metrics.
"/youtube/sponsorship" - Retrieve recent sponsored YouTube videos for a specified creator, including metadata and engagement metrics for evaluating sponsorship performance.
"/youtube/topics" - Retrieve all available YouTube topics with their categories and creator count. Topics are used for categorizing YouTube creators based on their content themes.
"/youtube/niches" - Retrieve all available YouTube niches with their categories and the number of creators. Niches provide more granular categorization of YouTube creators than topics.
"/youtube/subtitles/meta" - Retrieve available subtitle metadata for a video including `vssId`, `lang`, `langCode`. Returns a 404 error if the video does not exist.
"/youtube/subtitles/download" - Streams the specified YouTube video's subtitle data as a JSON file download with the filename format `{videoId}-{vssId}.json`. Returns a `content-disposition` attachment. Returns `404` if the video or subtitle ID is not found.
"/instagram/search" - Search for Instagram creators using advanced filters such as `displayName`, follower count, content performance, and more. Supports fuzzy search, pagination, and multiple filter combinations with up to 10 filters per request. The `creatorList` is, by default, sorted by `displayName`. Custom sorting can be applied using the `sortBy` parameter.
"/instagram/profile" - Retrieve basic profile information of a specified Instagram creator, including metadata such as follower count, account type, language, hashtags, and more.
"/instagram/contact" - Retrieve the contact information of the specified Instagram creator.
"/instagram/performance" - Retrieve advanced performance information of a specified Instagram creator, including post activity, follower growth, engagement metrics, and content performance.
"/instagram/performance-history" - Retrieve daily performance snapshots for a specified Instagram creator within a specific period, including upload activity, follower growth, engagement metrics, and post or reel performance. Dates are returned as Unix timestamps in milliseconds.
"/instagram/audience" - Retrieve audience demographic insights for a specified Instagram creator, including country distribution, gender breakdown, and age composition. This endpoint provides detailed audience analytics to help understand creator reach, evaluate audience alignment, and support data‑driven influencer selection and campaign targeting.
"/instagram/content-detail" - Retrieve the most recent Instagram image posts and reels of a specific creator, including publish time, post description, and engagement metrics. This endpoint enables users to fetch the latest content details for visual and textual analysis or further content-based operations.
"/instagram/sponsorship" - Retrieve sponsorship data for a specified Instagram creator, including current and past brand partnerships with performance metrics.
"/instagram/niches" - Retrieve all available Instagram niche categories and their creator counts. Niches categorize creators by content themes.

"/tiktok/profile" - Retrieve profile information of a specified TikTok creator, including metadata, statistics, hashtags, and content analysis.
"/tiktok/contact" - Retrieve the contact information of the specified TikTok creator.
"/tiktok/performance" - Retrieve advanced performance information of a specified TikTok creator, including post activity, follower growth, engagement metrics, and content performance.
"/tiktok/performance-history" - Retrieve daily performance snapshots for a specified TikTok creator within a specific period, including upload activity, follower growth, engagement metrics, and video performance. Dates are returned as Unix timestamps in milliseconds.
"/tiktok/audience" - Retrieve audience demographic insights for a specified TikTok creator, including country distribution, gender breakdown, and age composition. "/tiktok/content-detail" - Retrieve the most recent TikTok videos information of a specified creator, including publish time, video description, and engagement metrics.
